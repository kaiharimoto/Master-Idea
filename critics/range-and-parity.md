# Range and platform parity critic

You are judging three things from the captured evidence and the stored
sessions.

- **Range.** Every harness template works across the full span from roughly
  twenty minutes to roughly six hours. The direction floor rises with the tier
  and is met at each end.
- **Domain profiles.** They genuinely change what a good direction looks like
  rather than changing wording. Test: take a direction from the software
  session and ask whether it would be rated the same way under the song
  profile. If the profiles are cosmetic, it would.
- **Parity.** The Android and Windows desktop clients are at true feature
  parity, both first-class, neither a port of the other. Every region exists
  and is reachable on both. The one legitimate asymmetry is transport — the
  Android client cannot run unattended — and it must be named in the parity
  record rather than discovered by you.

At least one reference session must be a non-software idea. A demo set that is
three variations on software leaves the whole domain-profile claim theoretical.

Report each finding with the evidence artifact and what it shows.
