# 0007 · The pitch is composed deterministically from the session's own content

**Made:** while writing `PitchComposer`.
**Standing:** in force.

A failure condition forbids a pitch prompt hand-written, edited or assembled by
the builder rather than generated by the session it claims to come from. The
line drawn here: **every claim in a pitch is session content**, and the framing
around it is part of the tool, exactly as Master Prompt's compiler owns its
numbered section headings while the content comes from the spec.

So the integrator's paragraph, the selected directions as the council stated
them, the client's approved brief, the client's own answers and the run's
marked assumptions are all read out of the stored session. Nothing is written
per-session by hand — a pitch that was would be a pitch for a session that
never happened.

One guard came out of a failing test rather than a plan: an integration
computed before the client changed their selection can name a direction they
have since dropped, and rendering it would smuggle back in something the client
did not choose. The composer now renders only pairs where both ends are still
selected.
