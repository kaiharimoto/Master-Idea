# 0018 · The clerk drafts the brief, and the client's edit outranks it

**Made:** while walking the whole workflow before shipping.
**Standing:** in force.

The interview gate has always said that the scale verdict is "made by the
model from the vision, not chosen by the client from a menu", and that the
confirmed brief is *the tool's* restatement of the idea. What shipped was
neither. The restatement was four raw answers joined with full stops and a
`replaceAll('..', '.')`; the tier came from searching the appetite answer for
the words "year", "month" and "week". Both are placeholders, and a placeholder
in this position is load-bearing: the restatement becomes the constitution of
a deliberation the client will not attend, and the tier decides how long that
deliberation runs.

So an eleventh seat, the **clerk**, takes one turn before the sitting opens:
the client's answers in, a restatement and a scale verdict back. It holds a
licence of its own — `restate` — and neither `propose` nor `rate`, so the
independence invariant is untouched and `families_test` still holds.

Three things about how it is used.

**The tier is judged from the idea, not from the appetite.** The prompt says
so in as many words, because asked how long they have, everybody picks the
longest sitting — and a six-hour run on a postcard produces a hundred
directions about a postcard. A settled idea with a year behind it is a short
sitting; an open one with an afternoon is still open.

**The composed draft stays.** It is what the client sees the instant the last
question is answered — the tool moves first here as everywhere, so there is a
paragraph to correct rather than an empty box to fill — and it is the answer
on a device where no council can be reached. The clerk's draft replaces it
when it arrives, and a reply that cannot be read leaves it standing.

**The client's edit wins, always.** Once they have touched the field nothing
overwrites it: not a later draft, not a rebuild. Their approval is what makes
it constitution, and a tool that can overwrite the constitution while they are
writing it has misunderstood which of them is the client.

On a phone this is one more turn carried by hand — offered, not automatic,
because it is one turn before they have agreed to carry hundreds.
